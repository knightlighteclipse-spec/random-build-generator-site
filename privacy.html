Random Build Generator Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Information We Access

Random Build Generator may access information needed to provide requested gaming, Discord, and YouTube publishing features. The information accessed depends on the features a user chooses to use and the permissions granted.

YouTube and Google Data

If a user authorizes YouTube integration, the application may use the granted Google authorization to perform requested YouTube actions, such as uploading videos.

Google authorization credentials are used to maintain authorized access to the requested services. Users can revoke access through their Google Account security settings.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide and maintain the application's requested features, including video publishing and gaming tools. We do not sell personal information.

Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored by the application or its service providers as needed to operate enabled features. Reasonable measures should be taken to protect stored information and authorization credentials.

Third-Party Services

The application may interact with Google, YouTube, Discord, and other services. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning personal information, contact the developer through the support email provided in the application's Google OAuth configuration.
